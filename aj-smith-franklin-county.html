Not All Heroes Wear Capes: Franklin County Sheriff Saves the Day

By John Watts, Editor  |  The Heron Report  |  October 19, 2026
Franklin County Sheriff A.J. "Tony" Smith stops to help a stranded motorist whose vehicle fender came loose and lodged against the car. Photo: The Heron Report

FRANKLIN COUNTY, Fla. — When a driver's vehicle fender came loose and became firmly lodged against the car, Franklin County Sheriff A.J. "Tony" Smith stopped to help.

The encounter, shared with The Heron Report, offers a reminder that public service extends to the everyday problems that can leave someone needing a helping hand.

An unexpected vehicle problem can be stressful. Having someone stop and offer assistance brings reassurance — and when that person is the sheriff himself, it makes the encounter particularly meaningful.

That personal connection is one of the most treasured qualities of small-town life. Public officials are also neighbors, and their commitment to the community shows through the time and attention they give to individual citizens.

Smith's willingness to help reflects that neighbor-helping-neighbor spirit. Small acts of service can leave a lasting impression, building trust and reminding residents that someone cares about their well-being.

Not all heroes wear capes. Sometimes, they wear a sheriff's badge and stop when someone needs help.

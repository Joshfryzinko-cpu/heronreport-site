BaySavers: Local Volunteers Working to Protect the Future of Gulf and Franklin County Waters

The Heron Report | Local News  ·  November 18, 2026

Along Florida’s Forgotten Coast, our bays are more than beautiful scenery. They support commercial fishing, tourism, local businesses, and a way of life that generations of families have enjoyed.

One local nonprofit organization, BaySavers Florida, has dedicated years of volunteer efforts to protecting and improving these valuable waterways. Based in Port St. Joe, BaySavers focuses on environmental concerns affecting St. Joseph Bay, Apalachicola Bay, St. Andrew Bay, and surrounding waterways.

Local Volunteers Making a Difference

Led in part by longtime local advocate Frank “Dusty” May, BaySavers brings together volunteers who share a common goal: protecting the waters that make the Forgotten Coast such a special place to live.

What makes this organization particularly noteworthy is that its directors and board members serve without compensation. Their efforts focus on environmental research, public education, community involvement, and working with government agencies to explore long-term solutions.

BaySavers has spent years raising awareness about changes in freshwater circulation, sediment buildup, declining marine habitats, and the overall health of our coastal ecosystems. The organization has also advocated for scientific studies involving the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers to better understand how historical changes to regional waterways may have affected local marine life.

Protecting More Than Just the Water

Healthy bays mean healthy communities. From commercial fishermen in Franklin County to recreational scallopers in Gulf County, countless residents and visitors depend on the natural resources these waterways provide.

With recent concerns surrounding scallop populations, water quality, and marine life in St. Joseph Bay, organizations like BaySavers are helping keep these important environmental issues in the public conversation.

How You Can Support BaySavers

BaySavers is a nonprofit organization that accepts donations from individuals, businesses, and community members who want to support its mission. Donations help fund environmental research, public education, and advocacy efforts aimed at protecting the health of our bays.

Whether through financial contributions, volunteering, or attending community fundraisers, there are opportunities for residents to get involved. To make a donation or learn more about volunteering, visit baysaversfl.org/join-us, or see the organization's official site at baysaversfl.org.

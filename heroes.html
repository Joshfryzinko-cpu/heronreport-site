Escambia County EMS
EMS
Emergency Medical Services
Paramedic Phillip Cook
Escambia County EMS · Pensacola, Florida
Why He's a Hero

Paramedic Phillip Cook was named a 2026 Star of Life by the Florida Ambulance Association and honored at the Florida State Capitol in Tallahassee. Recognized for extraordinary service and unwavering commitment to patient care in Northwest Florida, Cook has gone above and beyond in every shift — embodying what it means to serve the Gulf Coast community.

“When every second counts, you want Paramedic Cook on your side.” — Local Pulse, Feb. 2026

Law Enforcement
Law Enforcement
Det. Robert Pence
Broward Sheriff’s Office · Fort Lauderdale, Florida
Why He's a Hero

Detective Robert Pence was named the Florida Sheriffs Association’s 2026 Law Enforcement Officer of the Year — the state’s highest law enforcement honor. In February 2026, he led the investigation into one of Broward County’s most complex cases: a triple homicide and child kidnapping. His cross-agency coordination and investigative skill brought violent offenders to justice and recovered firearms off Florida streets.

“Detective Pence delivered a year of service that stands apart from even the high standards expected of Florida law enforcement professionals.” — Florida Sheriffs Association, 2026
Source: Florida Sheriffs Association · 2026

Fire & Rescue
Capt. Joseph Falcone
Delray Beach Fire Rescue · Delray Beach, Florida
Why He's a Hero

Captain Joseph Falcone was awarded the 2026 Florida Fire Chiefs’ Association Safety and Health Award of Excellence. Over nearly 25 years, Falcone has led firefighter cancer prevention, fitness, behavioral health, and peer support programs — embedding wellness into department culture so that firefighters are encouraged to seek help before crisis strikes. He is a model for the entire state of Florida.

“For nearly 25 years, he has been a leader in the truest sense — one who cares deeply about the people he serves alongside.” — Chief Ronald Martin, Delray Beach FR
Source: Atlantic Ave Magazine · Sep. 2026

Okaloosa County EMS
EMS
Emergency Medical Services
Paramedic Andrew Kindle
Okaloosa County EMS · Fort Walton Beach, Florida
Why He's a Hero

Andrew Kindle, Community Paramedic with Okaloosa County EMS in Northwest Florida, was named the 2026 NAEMT Community Paramedic of the Year — a national honor. Working along the Florida Panhandle, Kindle goes beyond emergency calls to build lasting health support systems for vulnerable residents. His work has made a measurable difference in community health outcomes across Okaloosa County.

“Andrew Kindle exemplifies what it means to be a Community Paramedic — meeting patients where they are and building a healthier community one person at a time.” — NAEMT, 2026
Source: Niceville.com · Aug. 28, 2026
Law Enforcement
Law Enforcement
Deputies Michael & Christopher Wieczorek
Sarasota County Sheriff’s Office · Sarasota, Florida
Why They're Heroes

Deputies Michael and Christopher Wieczorek received Life Saving Awards from Sheriff Kurt A. Hoffman after responding to a drowning at an apartment complex. They found a person face-down and unresponsive in the deep end of a pool. Christopher entered the water and pulled the victim out. Michael arrived seconds later and assisted with CPR until EMS arrived. The victim survived.

“The men and women of the Sarasota County Sheriff’s Office go above and beyond — on and off duty — to keep our community safe.” — Sheriff Kurt A. Hoffman, Sep. 2026
Source: MySuncoast / WWSB · Sep. 17, 2026

Fire & Rescue
FF/PM Chris Martin
Seminole County Fire Dept. · Sanford, Florida
Why He's a Hero

Seminole County Firefighter/Paramedic Chris Martin received the American Red Cross First Responder Hero Award at the 2026 Greater Orlando Salute to Heroes Breakfast. A former UCF and NFL athlete, Martin has dedicated his career to pediatric drowning prevention — one of Florida’s leading causes of child death. He is also a recipient of the Florida Governor’s Florida Heroes Award.

“Martin’s courage, compassion and dedication to pediatric drowning prevention embody the mission of the American Red Cross.” — Red Cross Central Florida, Jun. 2026
Source: My Sanford Herald · Jun. 22, 2026
Inaugural Heroes · Week of
October 13, 2026
EMS
Emergency Medical Services
Gulf County EMS Team
Paramedics · Gulf County, Florida
Why They're Heroes

When Todd Hoffman's heart stopped for over 90 minutes after a cardiac arrest, Gulf County EMS crews responded alongside Sheriff's deputies and refused to quit. Working alongside Hoffman's wife — a nurse who started CPR immediately — they brought him back. Months later, Hoffman stood before them to say thank you in person.

"He didn't have a pulse, so I started CPR — shortly after the deputy came in with the AED." — Tannie Hoffman
Source: WJHG News · Feb. 4, 2026
Law Enforcement
Law Enforcement
Sgt. Brent Roberts
Bay County Sheriff's Office · Panama City, Florida
Why He's a Hero

Sergeant Brent Roberts was on patrol near Deerpoint Lake when a call came in. He spotted a man floating in the water, another screaming for help — and jumped in without hesitation. He pulled the drowning man to safety. The U.S. Attorney for the Northern District of Florida awarded Roberts the 2026 Hometown Heroes Award for his bravery.

"When searching for candidates, articles depicting Roberts's bravery quickly came to mind." — U.S. Attorney Jack Heekin
Source: MyPanhandle / WMBB · 2026
Fire & Rescue
Fire & Rescue
Pasco Co. Fire Rescue Station 26
Firefighters Ryan Garrity & Alexander Garcia · Pasco County, Florida
Why They're Heroes

On June 5, 2026, a man collapsed on a pickleball court in Pasco County. Bystanders started CPR. When Station 26 arrived, firefighter-paramedics Ryan Garrity and Alexander Garcia took over and saved his life. In October 2026, Pasco County Fire Rescue honored the crew with Life Saving Awards for two extraordinary rescues in a single month.

"Two different emergencies. Two extraordinary rescues. One mission: saving lives." — Pasco County Fire Rescue
Source: Hernando Sun · Oct. 9, 2026

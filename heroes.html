EMS
Photo: GetTheCoast.com / Okaloosa County EMS
Emergency Medical Services
Paramedic Andrew Kindle
Okaloosa County EMS · Fort Walton Beach, FL

Andrew Kindle, Community Paramedic with Okaloosa County EMS in Northwest Florida, was named the 2026 NAEMT Community Paramedic of the Year — a national honor. He goes beyond emergency calls to build lasting health support for vulnerable residents across the Panhandle.

“Meeting patients where they are and building a healthier community one person at a time.” — NAEMT, 2026
Source: Niceville.com · Aug. 28, 2026
Law Enforcement
Sarasota County Sheriff’s Office
Law Enforcement
Deputies Wieczorek
Sarasota County Sheriff’s Office · Sarasota, FL

Deputies Michael and Christopher Wieczorek received Life Saving Awards after responding to a drowning at a Sarasota apartment complex. They found a person face-down in the deep end of a pool, entered the water, pulled the victim out, and administered CPR until EMS arrived. The victim survived.

“The Sarasota County Sheriff’s Office goes above and beyond — on and off duty.” — Sheriff Kurt A. Hoffman
Source: MySuncoast / WWSB · Sep. 17, 2026

Photo: My Sanford Herald / Red Cross Central FL
Fire & Rescue
FF/PM Chris Martin
Seminole County Fire Dept. · Sanford, FL

Seminole County Firefighter/Paramedic Chris Martin received the American Red Cross First Responder Hero Award at the 2026 Greater Orlando Salute to Heroes Breakfast. A former NFL player, Martin has dedicated his career to pediatric drowning prevention — one of Florida’s leading causes of child death.

“Martin’s courage, compassion and dedication embody the mission of the Red Cross.” — Red Cross Central FL, Jun. 2026
Source: My Sanford Herald · Jun. 22, 2026

Paramedic Phillip Cook
Escambia County EMS · Pensacola, FL

Paramedic Phillip Cook was named a 2026 Star of Life by the Florida Ambulance Association and honored at the Florida State Capitol. Recognized for extraordinary service and unwavering commitment to patient care across Northwest Florida, Cook exemplifies what it means to serve the Gulf Coast community.

“When every second counts, you want Paramedic Cook on your side.” — Local Pulse, Feb. 2026

Photo: Florida Sheriffs Association, 2026
Law Enforcement
Det. Robert Pence
Broward Sheriff’s Office · Fort Lauderdale, FL

Detective Robert Pence was named the Florida Sheriffs Association’s 2026 Law Enforcement Officer of the Year — the state’s highest honor. He led the investigation into one of Broward County’s most complex cases: a triple homicide and child kidnapping, bringing violent offenders to justice and recovering firearms across Florida.

“Detective Pence delivered a year of service that stands apart from even the high standards expected of Florida law enforcement.” — Florida Sheriffs Association
Source: Florida Sheriffs Association · 2026

Photo: BocaPost / Delray Beach Fire Rescue
Fire & Rescue
Capt. Joseph Falcone
Delray Beach Fire Rescue · Delray Beach, FL

Captain Joseph Falcone was awarded the 2026 Florida Fire Chiefs’ Association Safety and Health Award of Excellence. Over nearly 25 years, Falcone has led firefighter cancer prevention, fitness, behavioral health, and peer support programs — embedding wellness into department culture so every firefighter knows help is available.

“For nearly 25 years, he has been a leader in the truest sense — one who cares deeply about the people he serves alongside.” — Chief Ronald Martin
Source: BocaPost · Sep. 2026
Inaugural Heroes · Week of
October 13, 2026
EMS
Gulf County, Florida
Emergency Medical Services
Gulf County EMS Team
Paramedics · Gulf County, Florida

When Todd Hoffman’s heart stopped for over 90 minutes after a cardiac arrest, Gulf County EMS crews responded alongside Sheriff’s deputies and refused to quit. Working with Hoffman’s wife — a nurse who started CPR immediately — they brought him back. Months later, Hoffman stood before them to say thank you.

“He didn’t have a pulse, so I started CPR — shortly after the deputy came in with the AED.” — Tannie Hoffman
Source: WJHG News · Feb. 4, 2026
Law Enforcement
Bay County Sheriff’s Office
Law Enforcement
Sgt. Brent Roberts
Bay County Sheriff’s Office · Panama City, FL

Sergeant Brent Roberts was on patrol near Deerpoint Lake when a call came in. He spotted a man floating in the water and another screaming for help — and jumped in. He pulled the drowning man to safety. The U.S. Attorney for the Northern District of Florida awarded Roberts the 2026 Hometown Heroes Award for his bravery.

“Articles depicting Roberts’s bravery quickly came to mind — he was an obvious choice.” — U.S. Attorney Jack Heekin
Source: MyPanhandle / WMBB · 2026
Fire & Rescue
Photo: Hernando Sun / Pasco County Fire Rescue
Fire & Rescue
Garrity & Garcia — Station 26
Pasco County Fire Rescue · Pasco County, FL

On June 5, 2026, a man collapsed on a pickleball court in Pasco County. Bystanders started CPR. When Station 26 arrived, firefighter-paramedics Ryan Garrity and Alexander Garcia took over and saved his life. In October 2026, Pasco County Fire Rescue honored the crew with Life Saving Awards.

“Two different emergencies. Two extraordinary rescues. One mission: saving lives.” — Pasco County Fire Rescue
Source: Hernando Sun · Oct. 9, 2026

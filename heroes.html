EMS
Emergency Medical Services
Gulf County EMS Team
Paramedics · Gulf County, Florida
Why They're Heroes

When Todd Hoffman's heart stopped for over 90 minutes after a cardiac arrest, Gulf County EMS crews responded alongside Sheriff's deputies and refused to quit. Working alongside Hoffman's wife — a nurse who started CPR immediately — they brought him back. Months later, Hoffman stood before them to say thank you in person.

"He didn't have a pulse, so I started CPR — shortly after the deputy came in with the AED." — Tannie Hoffman
Source: WJHG News · Feb. 4, 2026
Law Enforcement
Law Enforcement
Sgt. Brent Roberts
Bay County Sheriff's Office · Panama City, Florida
Why He's a Hero

Sergeant Brent Roberts was on patrol near Deerpoint Lake when a call came in. He spotted a man floating in the water, another screaming for help — and jumped in without hesitation. He pulled the drowning man to safety. The U.S. Attorney for the Northern District of Florida awarded Roberts the 2026 Hometown Heroes Award for his bravery.

"When searching for candidates, articles depicting Roberts's bravery quickly came to mind." — U.S. Attorney Jack Heekin
Source: MyPanhandle / WMBB · 2026
Fire & Rescue
Fire & Rescue
Pasco Co. Fire Rescue Station 26
Firefighters Ryan Garrity & Alexander Garcia · Pasco County, Florida
Why They're Heroes

On June 5, 2026, a man collapsed on a pickleball court in Pasco County. Bystanders started CPR. When Station 26 arrived, firefighter-paramedics Ryan Garrity and Alexander Garcia took over and saved his life. In October 2026, Pasco County Fire Rescue honored the crew with Life Saving Awards for two extraordinary rescues in a single month.

"Two different emergencies. Two extraordinary rescues. One mission: saving lives." — Pasco County Fire Rescue
Source: Hernando Sun · Oct. 9, 2026

Ghosts on the Coast Brings Halloween Spirit to Downtown Port St. Joe

By The Heron Report, Staff  ·  October 31, 2026
Reid Avenue packed with Halloween revelers at Ghosts on the Coast — Port St. Joe, October 31, 2026. Photo: The Heron Report

PORT ST. JOE — Halloween took center stage on Reid Avenue Saturday, Oct. 31, as Port St. Joe celebrated Ghosts on the Coast, a community event organized by the City of Port St. Joe and the Gulf County Chamber of Commerce.

The downtown celebration ran from 5:30 to 8:30 p.m., inviting families to enjoy Halloween in the heart of Port St. Joe. Reid Avenue closed to traffic ahead of the festivities, making room for the evening’s activities.

Francis Duty, 100, trick-or-treating on Reid Avenue.
Officer Kesterson with a young Spider-Man on Reid Avenue.

Trick-or-treating and a judged costume contest were among the evening’s activities. Costume divisions included children ages 0–3, 4–6 and 7–10, along with families, groups and pets, offering plenty of opportunities to show off some Halloween creativity.

Among the evening’s special guests was 100-year-old Francis Duty, who visited Port St. Joe for the celebration. Francis’s visit added a special touch to an occasion centered on bringing generations together.

A winning look on Reid Avenue — Marie Antoinette and Louis XVI arrive in Port St. Joe.

Beyond the candy and costumes, celebrations like Ghosts on the Coast give neighbors a reason to gather, families an opportunity to spend time downtown and children a chance to build memories close to home.

These events also take planning, time and community participation. The organizers and everyone who helped make the evening possible deserve recognition for keeping this hometown tradition going.

From its youngest trick-or-treaters to a special guest celebrating a century of life, Ghosts on the Coast offered a reminder of what makes community gatherings meaningful: people coming together.

The Heron Report thanks everyone who helped bring Halloween spirit to downtown Port St. Joe.

Seafood, Gulf Breezes, and Jimmy Buffett: Ralphie's Earns a Perfect 10

By John Watts, Editor, The Heron Report  |  October 24, 2026
Outstanding service at Ralphie's Fish Camp, Port St. Joe. — Photo: The Heron Report

PORT ST. JOE — Sometimes a meal captures exactly what makes life along the Forgotten Coast so enjoyable. Our recent visit to Ralphie's Fish House brought together beautiful weather, a cool Gulf breeze, and Jimmy Buffett tunes — the perfect setting to slow down and enjoy some seafood.

We ordered two mudslides, a dozen oysters, and mahi tacos, and the meal delivered.

The mahi tacos at Ralphie's — fresh, well-seasoned, and paired with crispy fries. — Photo: The Heron Report

The oysters and delicious mahi tacos paired perfectly with the relaxed coastal atmosphere, while the mudslides added a little vacation spirit to the outing.

Two mudslides at Ralphie's — the finishing touch on a perfect afternoon. — Photo: The Heron Report

The food presentation showed care, and the service matched the quality of the meal. With good music, pleasant weather, and a Gulf breeze rolling through, it was an experience worth savoring.

10 / 10
Food · Service · Presentation

Ralphie's Fish House earned a 10 out of 10 for food, service, and presentation. For an afternoon that felt like a little escape right here in Port St. Joe, it hit all the right notes.

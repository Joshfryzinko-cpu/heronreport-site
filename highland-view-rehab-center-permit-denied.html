City Denies Highland View Rehabilitation Center Permit

By John Watts, Editor | The Heron Report  ·  November 17, 2026
The Overlook at 1217 Highway 98 — the proposed site of the rehabilitation center.

HIGHLAND VIEW, FL — The City of Port St. Joe has denied the business permit for the proposed rehabilitation center at The Overlook on Highway 98, citing questions surrounding local ordinances and land-use requirements.

The decision involves City Ordinance 626, which addresses zoning, occupancy and safety standards for sober-living and recovery facilities.

County Attorney Addresses Legal Questions

County Attorney Jeremy Novak addresses the Board of County Commissioners, November 12, 2026.

During the November 12 County Commission meeting, County Attorney Jeremy Novak discussed the legal questions surrounding the facility. Gulf County is supporting the city’s position as officials prepare for a possible legal challenge.

It’s encouraging to see city and county officials working together to address community concerns while making sure local regulations are followed.

The Heron Report will continue monitoring developments as both governments work toward a resolution that respects the law and the interests of Highland View residents.

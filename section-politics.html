Trump Signs Executive Order to Cut Diesel Costs for Truckers and Farmers

President Trump signed an executive order removing federal restrictions on the use of dyed diesel fuel, a move the administration says will save truckers and farmers up to $100 per fill-up. The order eliminates a regulatory barrier that had prevented off-road diesel — taxed at a lower rate — from being used in commercial highway vehicles. Industry groups praised the move as immediate, concrete relief for the supply chain.

BCSO Sgt. Brent Roberts Receives Hometown Hero Award for Deerpoint Lake Water Rescue

Bay County Sheriff’s Office Sergeant Brent Roberts was honored by the U.S. Attorney’s Office with the 2026 Hometown Heroes Award after jumping into Deerpoint Lake to rescue a drowning man. Roberts was on patrol when he spotted an overturned kayak. He entered the water, lost consciousness briefly from exertion, and was later hospitalized — but the man survived. U.S. Attorney Jack Heekin said Roberts exemplifies the dedication of Florida’s law enforcement community.

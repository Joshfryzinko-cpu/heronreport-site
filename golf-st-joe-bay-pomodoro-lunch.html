A Perfect Sunday in Port St. Joe: Golf at St. Joe Bay and Lunch at Pomodoro

The Heron Report  |  Local News  |  November 22, 2026
A round at St. Joe Bay Golf Club on a cool, clear Sunday. — Photo: The Heron Report

PORT ST. JOE — Summer's over, the heat has let up, and the bugs aren't nearly as bad. It's the kind of Sunday that makes you want to grab your clubs and head to St. Joe Bay Golf Club.

That's exactly what we did. We played a round, enjoyed the cooler weather, and took our time on the course. Afterward, we headed to Pomodoro Italian Grill and Bar for lunch and another round, this time of drinks.

Pomodoro Italian Grill and Bar, Port St. Joe. — Photo: The Heron Report

The lasagna was delicious, and the Brussels sprouts came with Tupelo honey, bacon, and fried onions. The honey added a touch of sweetness to that salty bite.

Pomodoro's lasagna, served with toasted bread and parmesan crisps. — Photo: The Heron Report
Brussels sprouts with Tupelo honey, bacon, and fried onions. — Photo: The Heron Report

And the chicken Alfredo? To die for. That was my favorite, especially with a piece of toasted bread to soak up the sauce.

Chicken Alfredo, best enjoyed with a piece of toasted bread for the sauce. — Photo: The Heron Report

If you've been waiting for the summer heat to break, grab your clubs, get a round in at St. Joe Bay, and make time for lunch afterward. It's a good way to spend a Sunday right here in Port St. Joe.

About The Heron Report

A Heron Media Group publication based in Mexico Beach, Florida. Uncompromising journalism. No agenda but the truth.

Who We Are

The Heron Report is an independent investigative news publication founded by Heron Media Group in Mexico Beach, Florida. We cover the stories that powerful institutions would prefer stayed hidden — government accountability, surveillance technology, public records, and the people who hold power in Gulf County and across Florida.

We are not owned by a corporation, a political party, or a billionaire. We are accountable to our readers and to the public record.

What We Cover

Eagle Eye — Gulf County Watchdog

Our flagship investigative tool searches 675 Gulf County Board of County Commissioner meetings, 3.3 million words of public minutes, and 65+ official profiles for documented conflicts of interest, procurement irregularities, and legal flags. Search the record →

Florida Surveillance & Tech Accountability

We track how Florida law enforcement agencies use Flock Safety license plate readers, drone programs, facial recognition systems, and AI policing tools — and whether those systems are being used lawfully and transparently.

Government Accountability

We file public records requests, attend public meetings, and follow the money. We believe in Florida's Sunshine Law and use it daily.

Our Standards

  • We verify before publishing. Eagle Eye flags are sourced from confirmed public record only.
  • We correct errors promptly and transparently.
  • We do not accept advertising that compromises our editorial independence.
  • We name our sources when possible and protect them when necessary.

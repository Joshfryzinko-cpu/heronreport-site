About The Heron Report

The Heron Report is an independent news publication based in Mexico Beach, Florida, covering Gulf, Franklin, and Bay counties along Florida's Gulf Coast.

We cover politics, business, local news, and the people who make this stretch of the Forgotten Coast worth calling home.

Our goal is simple: report what's happening in our community, hold public officials accountable, and give residents the information they need.

Our Standards

  • We verify before publishing.
  • We correct errors promptly and transparently.
  • We do not publish unverified rumors.
  • We distinguish clearly between reporting and commentary.
  • We name our sources when possible and protect them when necessary.

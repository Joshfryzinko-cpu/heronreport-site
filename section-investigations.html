Federal Agents Search Rep. Ilhan Omar’s D.C. Home in Criminal Probe

📍 South Florida

Former CIA Official with Top-Secret Clearance Pleads Guilty to $194M Fraud Using Fake Classified Programs

A former senior CIA official pleaded guilty to defrauding the federal government of approximately $194 million over several years by fabricating classified programs and using the funds to acquire 298 gold bars, four luxury South Florida properties, high-end watches, and two BMW vehicles. The case, prosecuted by the Department of Justice, is among the largest known frauds by a federal employee with Top Secret clearance. Investigators described the scheme as elaborate and long-running.

📍 Washington, D.C.

White House Task Force Identifies $230 Billion in Improper Federal Payments and Fraud

Vice President JD Vance announced that the White House Task Force to Eliminate Fraud has identified approximately $230 billion in improper payments, systemic waste, and criminal fraud across federal agencies since its establishment. The task force has referred numerous cases to the Department of Justice. Vance called the findings “just the beginning” of a broader accountability drive targeting federal spending abuse. Critics argue the scope of reviews has also ensnared legitimate programs.

📍 Washington, D.C.

FEMA Inspector General: $13 Billion Wasted or Stolen in COVID-Era Pandemic Response Programs

A Department of Homeland Security Inspector General report found that FEMA’s pandemic-era programs wasted or lost approximately $13 billion to fraud, with inadequate identity verification and oversight enabling tens of thousands of fraudulent claims. The report found that the agency’s rapid scaling of emergency programs created systemic vulnerabilities exploited by organized fraud networks. The IG recommended sweeping reforms to identity verification protocols and real-time claims monitoring before the next major disaster.

📍 Orlando, FL

Orlando City Commissioner Regina Hill Arrested by FDLE on Elderly Exploitation and Fraud Charges

The Florida Department of Law Enforcement arrested Orlando District 5 City Commissioner Regina Hill, 58, on charges including elderly exploitation and fraud. FDLE investigators alleged that Hill exploited a vulnerable adult and used her position to facilitate financial fraud. Hill was a longtime elected official representing one of Orlando’s most economically challenged districts. Hill is presumed innocent until proven guilty. The case was referred to the State Attorney’s Office for prosecution.

📍 Miami, FL

Miami Man Charged With Defrauding Immigration System in Nationwide Health Care Fraud Takedown

Eduardo Javier Ibarra Arrowsmith, 61, a Cuban national and convicted felon living in Miami, was federally charged with immigration fraud and aggravated identity theft. Ibarra is accused of participating in a scheme that fraudulently certified naturalization applicants as disabled — thereby illegally exempting them from English language and civics requirements. The case was part of a national health care fraud enforcement action coordinated by USCIS and the Department of Justice.

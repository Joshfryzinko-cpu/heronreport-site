Toucan's in Mexico Beach Earns a 10/10 With Outstanding Lunch and Beautiful Views

Dining Review by John Watts, Editor | The Heron Report  ·  October 28, 2026
10/10
Toucan's Restaurant
Mexico Beach, FL
Toucan's Restaurant, Mexico Beach, Florida

MEXICO BEACH — Sometimes a lunch stop gives you every reason to start planning your next visit. That was my experience at Toucan's Restaurant in Mexico Beach, where an amazing chicken wrap, spectacular charbroiled oysters, and beautiful views made for a memorable afternoon.

Chicken wrap with crinkle-cut fries
Charbroiled oysters on the half shell

The chicken wrap was absolutely delicious and a standout lunch choice. Served alongside crinkle-cut fries, it was the kind of meal that makes you glad you stopped in.

"The charbroiled oysters were equally impressive — served on the half shell with beautifully browned tops and lemon on the side."

The charbroiled oysters were equally impressive. Served on the half shell with beautifully browned tops and lemon on the side, they looked inviting and tasted spectacular. They were a highlight of the meal and something I would happily order again.

Then there were the views. Taking a moment to enjoy the scenery added to the experience and made this lunch feel like a little getaway right here in Mexico Beach.

For this visit, Toucan's gets a 10 out of 10 from me. Between the food and the setting, there was plenty to enjoy — and plenty of reason to return.

Next time, I’ll be stopping in for dinner to try more of the menu. If lunch was any indication, I’m looking forward to seeing what comes out of the kitchen next.

Gulf County Commissioners Call Special Session Over Amendment 3 Budget Hit, Indian Pass Purchase

By John Watts, Editor | The Heron Report  ·  November 12, 2026
Commissioners met in the Donald H. Butler Commission Room at the Robert M. Moore Administration Building, Port St. Joe.

PORT ST. JOE — The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners convened a special meeting on Thursday, November 12, at 9 a.m. in the Donald H. Butler Commissioner Board Room, taking up four items: the county's fiscal exposure under newly passed Amendment 3, a possible purchase at Indian Pass, continued water-quality testing in St. Joseph Bay, and an administrative report and update from staff. Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr. presided.

Amendment 3 forces an emergency ordinance

The session followed The Heron Report's November 4 coverage of Amendment 3's passage, which Gulf County projects will cost its General Fund roughly $3.56 million in property-tax revenue once the expanded homestead exemption phases in. Commissioners used the special meeting to act on that exposure: by unanimous vote, the board authorized emergency advertisement of an ordinance addressing the shortfall, along with a first public reading. That reading and public hearing is scheduled for the board's regular meeting on Tuesday, November 24, at 9 a.m., also in the Donald H. Butler Commission Room, with a second reading and hearing to follow afterward. Residents may appear and be heard at both hearings; county officials note that anyone wishing to appeal a decision from either hearing will need their own verbatim record of the proceedings.

Indian Pass and St. Joseph Bay

The board also took up a possible county purchase at Indian Pass and heard an update on St. Joseph Bay water testing — the sampling program commissioners expanded to six bay locations in late October in partnership with the St. Andrew and St. Joseph Bays Estuary Program, after Florida Fish and Wildlife confirmed bloom-level red tide concentrations in the bay. The Heron Report is following up with the county clerk's office for the specifics of both items and will update this story as records become available.

Watch the meeting: Gulf County posts its commission meetings at the county's own past meeting links page. The Heron Report also recorded the full session and will share it on our Facebook page for residents who couldn't attend — check back here and on our Facebook page for the video.

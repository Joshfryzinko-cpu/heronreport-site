Port St. Joe Police Department Celebrates Promotions, Welcomes New Officer

By The Heron Report, Staff  ·  November 1, 2026
Left to right: Lt. Jeremy Chase, Sgt. Jerry Lee, Officer Jessica Page and Chief Jake Richard at the Port St. Joe Police Department. Photo: PSJPD

PORT ST. JOE — The Port St. Joe Police Department is celebrating two promotions and welcoming an experienced officer to its team.

Jeremy Chase has been promoted to lieutenant, and Jerry Lee has been promoted to sergeant. Chief Jake Richard invited the community to join him in congratulating both officers as they step into their new leadership roles.

The department also welcomed Officer Jessica Page, who brings 15 years of prior law enforcement experience to PSJPD. Her arrival marks the beginning of a new chapter serving the residents, businesses and visitors of Port St. Joe.

The promotions represent important career milestones for Chase and Lee, with each taking on the responsibilities that accompany a supervisory position. Alongside Page’s arrival, the announcements offer the community an opportunity to recognize the officers serving their city.

Chief Richard’s recognition of these milestones is a welcome acknowledgment of his team.

The Heron Report congratulates Lt. Jeremy Chase and Sgt. Jerry Lee on their promotions and welcomes Officer Jessica Page to Port St. Joe. We wish all three success in their new roles.

📍 Manatee County, FL

Nopetro Energy Breaks Ground on $50M Renewable Gas Plant at Manatee Landfill

Florida-based Nopetro Energy and Johnson Controls broke ground Oct. 9 on a $50 million renewable natural gas facility at the Lena Road Landfill in east Manatee County. The plant will capture biogas currently flared into the atmosphere—turning decomposing waste into sellable energy. Manatee County contributes the land and gets back at least $883,000 in annual royalties, with the potential for $2.1 million, at zero cost to taxpayers. The facility will also eliminate carbon dioxide equivalent to 5.3 million gallons of gasoline each year.
